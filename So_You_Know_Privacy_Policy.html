Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 August 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the So You Know mobile application ("So You Know", "the App") handles your information.

So You Know is published on Google Play by Jupiter VII A and is operated by Shaun Maxwell Ryan ("we", "us", or "our"). By using the App, you agree to this policy.

1. The Short Version

So You Know does not have a server, does not use accounts, and does not collect, transmit, or share any personal information. Everything you do in the App, the cards you pin, the people you save, anything you write, stays only on your device. We cannot see it, and we never receive it.

2. Information We Collect

None. The App does not require or offer account creation, does not use sign-in of any kind, and has no backend server to send information to. There is nothing for us to collect.

3. What Stays On Your Device

The App stores the following information locally, on your device only:

  • Which conditions you've selected and which cards you've pinned
  • Any people you've saved for tailoring cards to specific relationships
  • Any custom cards you've written yourself
  • Your collections and appearance preferences

None of this ever leaves your device automatically. It is not backed up to any server, because no server exists to back it up to.

4. Encrypted Backup

The App includes an optional backup feature that lets you generate a passphrase-protected, encrypted backup code you can save or transfer yourself. This is entirely user-initiated: the App does not send this code anywhere. If you choose to share it, that is your own action, not something the App does automatically. The backup is encrypted such that it cannot be read without the passphrase you set.

5. Payments

Premium subscriptions are handled directly by Google Play's billing system. We do not receive or store your payment details. Google's handling of payment information is covered by Google's own privacy policy.

6. Advertising

So You Know carries no ads, and never will. There is no advertising SDK anywhere in the App.

7. Analytics and Tracking

The App contains no analytics, crash reporting, or tracking software of any kind. Nothing about how you use the App is measured, recorded, or sent anywhere.

8. Data Deletion

Because everything is stored only on your device, deleting your data is entirely in your hands. Uninstalling the App removes everything. There is no account to delete and no server-side copy to request removal of, because none exists.

9. Children's Privacy

So You Know is intended for adults and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children, and as described above, we do not collect personal information from anyone.

10. Third-Party Services

The only third party involved in the App is Google, solely for processing subscription payments through Google Play's billing system. See Google's privacy policy for details on their handling of payment information.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Significant changes will be reflected by updating the date at the top of this page.
